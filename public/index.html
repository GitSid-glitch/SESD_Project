Role Workflows

Each role sees a different part of the operating system.

Admin

Owns access and governance

Reviews users, changes roles, tracks high-level activity, and keeps the workspace structured.

Manager

Owns planning and coordination

Creates projects, schedules sprints, adds team members, and assigns tasks to the right people.

Member

Owns execution and updates

Works from a personal task board, updates progress, shares blockers, and comments directly on tasks.

How Work Moves

Projects become sprints, sprints become tasks, tasks become conversations.

1

Set up the workspace

Admins define who can do what. Managers create delivery spaces and bring the right teammates in.

2

Assign and execute

Managers create work with deadlines and ownership. Members get a focused board of what they should do next.

3

Communicate on the work

Status changes and comments keep everyone aligned without leaving the task context.
